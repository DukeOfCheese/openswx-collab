# Code of Conduct

openswx and openswx-collab are built by engineers, students, hobbyists and people who write software, some of them working with
AI agents. We want everyone to be able to take part, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic; everyone started somewhere.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and accept it the same way.
- Credit other people's work, and follow the clean-room rule in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- If you submit work produced with an AI tool, you are responsible for it as if you wrote it: check it, test
  it, and make sure you may contribute it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Threats, or encouraging others to harm anyone.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/DukeOfCheese/openswx-collab/security/advisories/new) (choose it
even though it is not a security issue: it is the private channel), or by GitHub's *Report content* on the
comment. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and warn, suspend or ban participants, depending on
how serious and how repeated the behaviour is. Decisions are explained to the person concerned where that is
safe.
